## Model (rubric: Model 6)

P(x, y) = τ(x, y; λ, μ, ρ) Pois(x; λ) Pois(y; μ), with τ = 1 − λμρ (0-0), 1 + λρ (0-1), 1 + μρ (1-0),
1 − ρ (1-1) and 1 otherwise. Admissible range: max(−1/λ, −1/μ) ≤ ρ ≤ min(1/(λμ), 1) for every match.
The corrections cancel over the full grid, so τ conserves probability. The log-likelihood is the Poisson
log-likelihood plus Σ log τ. It is maximised jointly over the log-strengths, log γ and ρ (sign free, box
[−0.5, 0.5]) by L-BFGS-B with an **analytic gradient**, checked against central differences (error about 1e-8).
The fit starts from the Poisson MLE with ρ = 0. The fitted ρ is checked to lie inside the admissible range
(pooled range [−0.226, +0.214]). The pooled fit has season-specific α and β, one γ and one ρ: 431
parameters, 4,180 matches. One pooled refit takes about 25 ms, so no profiling shortcut was needed for the
bootstrap. A profile likelihood in ρ is still computed (61 grid points) as a cross-check and gives an LR
interval.

## What disagrees with `review/final_plan_by_rubric.md` (reported as it came out)

- The plan expected ρ ≈ −0.05 and a gain of "about 5 nats". ρ̂ = −0.051 as expected. The **log-likelihood**
  gain is 2.64 nats; the 5.27 figure is the LR statistic (twice the gain). The plan's "5 nats" is probably the
  LR statistic.
- The plan's note was right that 0-0 is **over**-predicted pooled (263 observed vs 283.1 expected). Negative
  ρ makes this worse: DC expects 305.3, a Pearson residual of −2.42. The pooled ρ is negative anyway because
  it fits 1-1 (455 obs vs 431.5 Poisson, 453.3 DC) and 1-0/0-1 (fewer than Poisson). The plan's sentence
  "ρ may come out small or positive" did not hold. ρ is small, negative, and its CI excludes 0, but only
  just (LR p = 0.022).
- The plan's "say no cell exceeds 2 SD if that holds": **it does not hold.** Pooled over 11 seasons, 4 of 17
  cells exceed |2|. The largest is 2-2 at +3.33, and a max |r| this large happens with probability 0.009
  under the fitted model. The misfit sits mostly outside the four cells τ touches: 2-2 and 3-1 come too
  often, 4+ too rarely. Together with the pooled dispersion 0.948 (below the Poisson range [0.969, 1.031])
  and too few home 0s and 6+, the pattern is mild **under-dispersion of home goals**. Dixon–Coles cannot
  fix that. This belongs in "What this does not show".
- Per-season dispersion 0.880..1.090 matches the plan's quoted 0.88–1.09.
